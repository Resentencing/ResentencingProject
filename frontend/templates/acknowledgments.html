Acknowledgments

{{ rad_page_header_end() }} {{ rad_section(flush=true) }}

This project is the result of years of collaboration, research, and commitment from students, faculty, and community partners at Sonoma State University and beyond. We are deeply grateful to the many individuals who contributed their time, ideas, and lived experience to advancing transparency in resentencing and expanding access to justice. We recognize that this work builds on the experiences and includes the input of people directly impacted by incarceration (including many students named below). Their advice, resilience, and advocacy continue to guide the purpose of this project.
