System Trends Viewer{{ ckh_note('how_to_use.system_trends') }}

Public summary charts. No names or personal details. No login. Includes case progression (considered, letters sent, resentenced), not a single fixed dashboard.

  • What share of cases are resentenced?
  • Which counties send the most cases?
  • How do outcomes vary by county, institution, or group?

Open the System Trends Viewer

Signed-in tools (Tool Hub){{ ckh_note('how_to_use.case_level') }}

After you log in at the Tool Hub, use these tabs: Look up a case, Compare groups, Ask a question, and (for staff) Check tracking log. Please follow usage guidelines for sensitive data.

  • Search and download court letters for one case or many matches
  • Build comparison charts with filters (action taken, sentence type, county, year, race)
  • Ask questions in plain language; always confirm important facts against the PDF
