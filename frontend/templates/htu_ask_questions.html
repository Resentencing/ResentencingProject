In the Tool Hub, open the Ask a question tab.

Type a question in plain language about case records and CDCR court letters. The assistant works best for counts and comparisons (how many, which county, outcomes by cohort). For one person, use Look up a case instead. See sample questions you can try or adapt.

What data this uses

See the variables page for details. This tool uses information from:

  • CDCR case records (what happened in the case)
  • Court letters (why CDCR made the recommendation)
  • Basic case details like county, dates, and outcomes
{{ icon_warning() }}

Some letters may include personal information such as behavior and discipline history; programs, work, and education; support from staff or family; and health or background information.

Important note about AI responses

The assistant uses artificial intelligence. It may sometimes give incorrect, incomplete, or misleading answers (sometimes called hallucinations): it can sound confident even when the answer is wrong.

Always verify important facts against the letter PDF or a case lookup before you rely on an answer in court, advocacy, or press.

Prefer questions about groups ("how many in Sacramento County") over guessing about one person from the AI alone.
