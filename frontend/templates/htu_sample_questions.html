What questions can this data answer?

This data can help answer different kinds of questions about resentencing. It can show patterns and relationships, but it cannot prove cause and effect. That means it can answer questions like: Do certain counties have higher success rates? Do outcomes differ across groups? What factors appear more often in successful cases? These are correlations, not proof that one thing caused another. For example, if one group has a higher success rate, the data can show that pattern, but it cannot prove why it happened. To test whether differences are meaningful or likely due to chance, additional statistical methods may be needed, such as chi-squared tests or other analyses. This tool is designed to help you identify patterns and ask better questions, not to make final causal conclusions.

  • Ask descriptive questions to understand what is happening, like how many cases were resentenced or how many years were reduced.
  • Ask comparison questions to see differences between groups, like how outcomes vary by county, race, or cohort.
  • Ask trend questions to see how things change over time, like whether resentencing rates are increasing or decreasing.
  • Ask case-specific questions to look at one person's case, including what happened, what was recommended, and what the outcome was.
  • Ask pattern questions to understand what factors appear in successful or unsuccessful cases.
