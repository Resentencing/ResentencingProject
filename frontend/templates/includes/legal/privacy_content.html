1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the RAD team (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you request access to or use our 1172.1 resentencing data tool (the “Service”). The Service helps mission-aligned users explore and analyze public-records-based information related to California Penal Code § 1172.1 resentencing. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

Related document: Terms of Service.

2. Who we are and contact

We are the Resentence and Decarcerate (RAD) team. We are not a law firm; we operate a research and advocacy-support tool.

Contact (privacy and access): ResentenceDecarcerate@gmail.com

3. Where the Service runs

  • Public website / tool: user-facing pages (currently at rscap.pythonanywhere.com).
  • Application / API hosting: PythonAnywhere apps.
  • Access request: Google Form + linked Sheet + Apps Script at the current access form.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information you provide (access request)

When you submit our Google Form, we collect fields that match our live form, including:

  • Timestamp (automatic)
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Role or affiliation
  • California Resentencing Coalition question
  • Agreement acknowledgment

Administrative columns (approval status, notes, domain checks, link sent status) may be added in the linked spreadsheet for internal operations.

4.2 Access links (magic links)

We do not store routine user passwords. Access may be granted via a unique access link tied to your email. We may log when a link is used and which email it is associated with for security and administration.

4.3 Automatic / technical data

  • Hosting providers: IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and similar server logs as described in provider policies. Current providers include PythonAnywhere, Netlify (if used for static/frontend delivery), and Streamlit Community Cloud (for the hosted RAG app, if enabled).
  • Google services: Forms, Sheets, and Gmail operations under Google policies. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • AI features (if enabled): prompts and relevant query text may be sent to OpenAI to generate responses. See OpenAI Privacy Policy.

4.4 Staff / operations

Project staff may use additional development and sync tools; those are for operations and do not expand coalition user Form collection beyond what is described here.
