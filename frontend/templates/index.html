How metrics are defined · data quality

Funnel (poster view). Considered = all letter records in the database. Letters sent = records with court/outcome text filled in (action_taken). Resentenced = subset flagged by rule-based keywords (resentenced, released, grant, approved, recalled). This mirrors reporting vocabulary; it is not a randomized controlled outcome.

Missingness. Incomplete logs, not-yet-ingested letters, or blank court fields reduce later funnel stages. County and outcome charts omit or group sparse categories. Race/ethnicity views use values merged from the active race spreadsheet when available.

Stacked outcome charts. Green = favorable outcome (same keyword rule as resentenced/favorable above). Gray = all other or unknown outcomes.
