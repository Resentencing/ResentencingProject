Check tracking log

Compares CDCR’s 1170(d) Main Tracking Log (Excel) with letter records in our database. We match on CDCR number first, then court case number. Missing from database means the log lists a letter we do not have on file yet (often still requested from CDCR). Counts use the approved-and-sent scope: letter date filled, sent to the Secretary, and Secretary’s decision = Approved.
