1170(d) Log vs. PDFs on Disk

Compares the 1170(d) Main Tracking Log (Excel) against letter PDFs on disk (archive, upload queue, and processed staging). Matching uses CDCR # or Case # parsed from filenames. Letters marked missing are approved on the log but we do not have a matching PDF yet — candidates for CDCR re-request. Approved-and-sent scope only: letter created, sent to the Secretary, and Secretary approved (declined / no-letter rows excluded). Queued PDFs count as received even if not yet in the public database.
