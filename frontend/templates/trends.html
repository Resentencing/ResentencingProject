Letters sent to court

--

{{ icon_mail() }}

Letters sent to court counts letter records that have a court response in our database (the Action Taken field is filled in). It means the case moved past referral—not necessarily the exact mail date.

Cases resentenced

--

{{ icon_courthouse() }}

What is a “case” here? One tracked letter event in our database (not always one person). Cases resentenced is the smaller group where Action Taken shows a favorable court outcome—resentenced, released, granted, approved, or recalled. Most letter records never reach this stage; 649 is not everyone considered.

Years reduced

--

{{ icon_calendar() }}

Years reduced is the total sentence time removed across favorable outcomes only (same resentenced/released/grant group). Records with blank or unknown reduction are excluded.

Letter records

--

{{ icon_document() }}

Letter records are case-level rows extracted from resentencing letter PDFs in our database. The count can exceed individuals because some people have more than one letter or case on file.

Individuals (approx.)

--

{{ icon_people() }}

Individuals (approx.) deduplicates letter records into distinct people using CDCR number, case number, name, or PDF when those fields differ. It is an estimate, not a legal identity count.

Counties

--

{{ icon_map_pin() }}

Counties is the number of distinct county values among letter records—where cases in the dataset are attributed geographically.
