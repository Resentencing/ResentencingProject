Missing Letters — Log vs PDFs on Disk

Compares the 1170(d) Main Tracking Log against letter PDFs on disk: archive, upload queue (uploads/), and processed (processed/). Matching is by CDCR # or Case # parsed from each PDF filename (same rules as OCR ingest). Only approved-and-sent rows are counted: Date Letter Created filled, Date Letter Sent to Secretary's Office filled, and Secretary's Decision = Approved. If the PDF is queued or not in the database yet, it still counts as have the letter.

Missing PDF = on the tracking log (letter was generated and approved) but no matching PDF in any of those folders.
